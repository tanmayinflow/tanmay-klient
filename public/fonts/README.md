# Optional typography fonts

Book is derived from Adobe Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3; Clear is derived from IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Serif. All are licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. See book-serif-OFL.txt, book-sans-OFL.txt, clear-sans-OFL.txt and clear-serif-OFL.txt.

The Latin/Czech subsets and weight range 400–700 are modified builds. Reserved names are replaced with Tanmay Book Serif, Tanmay Book Sans, Tanmay Clear Sans and Tanmay Clear Serif. Copyright and license metadata are retained. No endorsement by Adobe or IBM is implied.

Official sources:
- https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif/tree/80d3f8894c09c937bebfa9011247d2e1c79fd6f4
- https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans/tree/87b37a2daaed80fcb8e8ccb0085c4d72ddade12e
- https://github.com/IBM/plex/tree/763c36ef9117782905ae010056dfbe8fd2653a25

Existing brand fonts and their original OFL.txt are preserved separately. Font files are served on this origin without third-party font requests.
